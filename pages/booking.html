🎾

Toys & treats

👟

Suitable footwear

It's a countryside field — wellies or walking boots are recommended in wet weather.

Before you book — dog requirements

  • We welcome dogs of all ages
  • All dogs must be up to date with vaccinations and worming, or have valid titer test results. Kennel cough vaccination is strongly recommended
  • Dogs in season are welcome
  • Dogs must be monitored by someone at all times
  • XL Bullies and other listed breeds are welcome — please ensure you comply with current legislation
  • Maximum 8 dogs per booking — contact us if you'd like to bring more
🚗

Cars & parking

To keep access safe and the lane clear:

  • A maximum of 2 cars is allowed for any booking
  • All cars must park inside the gate in the designated parking area
  • There is no parking outside on the lane or verge

Please arrange to arrive in no more than two vehicles per session.

About the horses

Paw Paradise is set within working countryside. Horses from surrounding properties may occasionally pass by — this is outside of our control. However, we also have our own horses that we ride past on the same property, though always on the other side of the fence from the dog field.

All of the horses in the area are very well-accustomed to dogs of all temperaments. Your dog remains in a fully enclosed, secure field throughout their session, and the horses will not enter the field.

For the vast majority of visitors, including those with reactive dogs, this isn't something to worry about. If you have a genuine concern about horses riding past and would prefer we plan around your session, please email us before your visit, and we will do our best to accommodate you.
