For sensitive dogs

A calm, secure space for sensitive dogs

Some dogs find the world a bit too much – busy parks, unexpected dogs and off-lead greetings they didn't ask for. Paw Paradise Lovedean is designed with those dogs in mind.

  • Your slot is for you and your dogs only – no shared sessions
  • A double-gated "airlock" entrance and continuous 6-foot fencing help keep even determined escape artists safely contained

Friendly for reactive, nervous and rescue dogs – because they deserve a slice of paradise too.

The field

A large dog field for safe, off-lead adventure

We've shaped Paw Paradise Lovedean as a mini adventure field so every visit feels rewarding for both body and brain.

  • A large, fully-enclosed dog field for zoomies, recall practice or a gentle potter
  • Trees, wildflowers and rustic obstacles and activities made using our own resources where possible

It's not just exercise – it's safe, off-lead adventure time.

Is it right for you?

Who Paw Paradise Lovedean is perfect for

  • Reactive or nervous dogs who struggle in busy public spaces
  • Rescue dogs building confidence at their own pace
  • High-energy dogs who need secure, off-lead sprint time
  • Families wanting a safe, private space to enjoy time with their dogs
  • Owners working on recall and training without distractions

If you've ever cut a walk short because a loose dog appeared from nowhere, or avoid certain places altogether, Paw Paradise Lovedean is for you.

Family business

Built by dog owners, for dog owners

We're a family business living on site at Paw Paradise Lovedean, on the edge of the South Downs National Park — a place we love. We have dogs and horses of our own, and we care deeply about animal welfare.

We set out to create somewhere we'd genuinely want to bring our own dogs — and we're committed to investing in the things that make a real difference: the features, the facilities and the little details that clients actually want to see and use.
