Paw Paradise Lovedean – secure, private dog paddock

A large, fully enclosed private dog field in the South Downs National Park, designed so all dogs can truly relax off-lead, whether they're reactive, rescue, sensitive, have selective recall, or just want some space to run around on their own or with their friends.

Why choose us

A secure, private dog field in Lovedean

Every 50-minute session is exclusive use, so the whole field is yours: no other dogs, no surprises. Open seven days a week, all year round, on the edge of Lovedean in the South Downs National Park.

🔒

Exclusive use

Book the whole field for you and your dogs. No shared sessions and no unexpected dogs.

🚧

Fully enclosed

6-foot deer fencing all the way round and a double-gated entrance, so even determined escape artists stay safely inside.

🌿

More than a flat field

Trees, wildflowers, a sandpit and rustic obstacles made from our own resources, for zoomies, sniffing and recall practice.

🛖

Shelter and facilities

A field shelter for showers and quiet breaks, with seating, fresh water and bowls, bins and poo bags on site.

🕐

Long opening hours

7:00am – 9:00pm in summer and 8:00am – 9:00pm in winter, seven days a week.

🚗

Parking by the field

Off-road parking inside the gate, right next to the paddock, for up to two cars per booking.

Who it's for

Somewhere every dog can relax off-lead

Some dogs find busy parks too much. Others just need space to run. Paw Paradise Lovedean is for both.

  • Reactive, nervous or rescue dogs who need space away from other dogs
  • Dogs with selective recall who can't be let off the lead elsewhere
  • High-energy dogs who need a safe place to sprint
  • Owners working on recall and training without distractions
  • Friends and families wanting a private space to enjoy time with their dogs

If you've ever cut a walk short because a loose dog appeared from nowhere, this is for you.

Family business

Built by dog owners, for dog owners

We're a family business and we live right next to the field. We have dogs and horses of our own, and we care deeply about animal welfare.

We set out to create somewhere we'd genuinely want to bring our own dogs — and we're committed to investing in the things that make a real difference: the features, the facilities and the little details that clients actually want to see and use.

All breeds welcome

Every dog deserves a paradise

We welcome dogs of all breeds and sizes – including XL Bullies and dogs subject to the Dangerous Dogs Act, providing they are handled responsibly and in line with current legislation.
